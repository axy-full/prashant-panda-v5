Chupa Chups - Carrom

Perfetti Van Melle · Ogilvy · Jamic Films · Dir. Nikhil Rao

The brief

A carrom board, a crowded room and one sweet. The film had to land a joke that reads in a single wide, without a word of dialogue to lean on.

The cut

Most of the coverage was close. The film only worked once the wide was held long enough for the room to react, so the edit resists cutting in and lets the beat play out where the audience can see everyone at once.

The result

One Gold, two Silver and a Bronze at the Good Ads Matter Awards 2026, and a Kyoorius Creative Awards win.
